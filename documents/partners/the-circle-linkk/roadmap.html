What The Circle assesses

Company stage and business model · product and commercial traction · revenue, growth potential and scalability · team experience and execution capacity · fundraising objectives and investor readiness · geographic focus and international potential · the support the company actually needs

The Web3 Corridor fit

The Seed Accelerator asks for a GCC focus or international scalability. India–UAE corridor startups fit that by design, which makes them IBA's strongest accelerator referrals.

How to proceed

Decisions for IBA, and the reply

Decide before the partner call

  1. Commercial termsReferral fee or revenue share on Startup Member and accelerator referrals. Club Class already bundles Global Member (US$990) inside US$2,500.
  2. How the levels are presentedThe Circle says it is now part of IBA's membership offering. Show the levels as "the IBA pathway, with The Circle", or as The Circle's own programmes?
  3. Member data and consentReferrals share company details. Add consent to the enquiry and brochure forms before the first referral.
  4. PromisesEvery referral and every page says it plainly: admission is selective; introductions and fundraising outcomes are not guaranteed.
  5. LINKK scopeConfirm A, B and D first; agree whether ecosystem partnership carries any cost or commitment.

Note: the email says "IAB" throughout. Ask that partner materials use IBA, India Blockchain Alliance.

Suggested reply

Thank you for the detailed overview of The Circle's programmes and the introduction to LINKK Global.

The Circle. The levels and criteria are clear, and we will use them to refer members. Our Club Class already includes Global Member. Before the first referrals to Startup Member and the accelerators, we would like to agree the referral process, how outcomes are reported back to us, and the commercial terms. Could you also confirm the brand use on co-branded material? It is IBA, India Blockchain Alliance.

LINKK Global. We would like to start with three areas: the ecosystem partnership (A), India–UAE/GCC market expansion (B) and joint sessions and roundtables (D), with a first India → UAE market-entry session in Q2. Investor and corporate connections (C) and venture building (E) could follow once those are running.

Could we set a call in the week of 7 December to agree next steps?
