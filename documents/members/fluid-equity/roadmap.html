Your 12-month roadmap: the year at a glance

Membership is a journey, not a static subscription: every stage is gated, and every gate is earned. The year runs in four stages, each closing with a gate we review together before the next stage opens.

Q1 · Dec 2026 – Feb 2027
Foundation & Onboarding

Get set up, verified and visible inside the ecosystem, and agree what success looks like for the year.

  • Official Club Badge, verifiable vault and directory listing
  • Baseline on the KPI framework; 12-month goals agreed
  • GTM blueprint started
  • First pitch deck review
  • First community event and roundtable
  • Membership spotlight on IBA LinkedIn

Q2 · Mar – May 2027
Strategy & Positioning

Turn the draft into a go-to-market plan you can act on, with the regulatory picture clear.

  • GTM blueprint completed: product lifecycle, business plan, expansion strategy
  • Regulatory mentoring for your target markets
  • Business plan review
  • Ecosystem introductions sought through the directories and roundtables
  • Roundtables and member roundups
Gate 2 · Strategy setGTM blueprint signed off, target markets and regulatory steps clear.

Funding Readiness & Visibility

Get investor-ready and raise Fluid Equity's profile across IBA's channels.

  • Growth advisory: deck and plan reviews, tailored funding preparation
  • Data room completed in your vault, using the data-room guidance
  • Your podcast recording
  • Media promotion: podcast, live TV, crypto news media
  • Investor and industry networking sessions
Gate 3 · Investor-readyFinal deck, business plan and data room ready to share; funding ask defined.

Market Access & Growth

Put Fluid Equity in front of the corridor and convert relationships into opportunities.

  • Showcase at community events and roundtables
  • Dubai co-hosted event, at member pricing
  • Follow-up on potential deal-flow and partner opportunities
  • Preferential pricing on any Corridor services you need
  • Year-end review against the Q1 baseline
Gate 4 · Year one completeResults reviewed against goals; plan for year two agreed.

How to proceed

Getting started: the next two weeks

Before 1 December

  1. Send the welcome letter and invoiceWelcome pack to Fluid Equity, with this roadmap attached.
  2. Name the leads on both sidesOne IBA lead and one Fluid Equity lead own the roadmap and the gate reviews.
  3. Share the onboarding form and Membership AgreementSo both can be completed by 5 December.
  4. Book the welcome callFor the week of 7 December.

On the welcome call

  1. Walk through this roadmapConfirm the four stages and the Gate 1 criteria.
  2. Book Q1's three mentorship blocksDecember, January and February, four hours each.
  3. Agree how we workA short monthly check-in inside the mentorship hours; updates by email between.
  4. Hand over the starter kitKPI framework, playbooks, templates and the data-room checklist.

What we need from Fluid Equity

  • Contact details for the lead and billingName, title, email and address.
  • Company documents for the vaultIncorporation documents, current deck, business plan or one-pager, key financials.
  • Brand assets for the LinkedIn spotlightLogo, a one-line description and a link.
  • Three goals for the yearA first view to bring to the welcome call; we sharpen them in January.
How the gates work. At the end of each quarter we review the gate together. When its criteria are met, the next stage opens; if something is outstanding, we agree what closes it and carry it into the next quarter's first weeks. The gates keep the year moving at Fluid Equity's pace, and make sure each stage builds on the last.
