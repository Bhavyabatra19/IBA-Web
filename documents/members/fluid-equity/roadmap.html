A 12-month Club Class roadmap for Fluid Equity, built around where the company is today: the model designed, counsel engaged, the pilot fund forming and the first building ahead.

Fluid Equity today

Fluid Equity builds rent-to-equity structures for housing. Investors (Class A) buy the building with no bank loan and are paid first, when the building earns it. Residents (Class B) pay ordinary rent and build a capped stake of up to a quarter of the home, without a down payment or a mortgage. The stake is recorded, and it is not a coin.

Fluid EquityCore. No loan: part of the rent becomes a share of the home.
Fluid EdgeBuildings that already have a mortgage. The lender is paid first.
Fluid EntryPublic and authority housing. A credit, not a share.
BaseVergennes, Vermont · eastern US pilot
StageLegal first: counsel engaged, no building bought yet
CapitalPilot fund; Class A investors at 6%, paid when the building earns it
TeamAndrew Lee and Peter Stevens, co-founders · Britt Crompton, CMO · Anthony Craig, blockchain educator

How to proceed

Getting started: the next two weeks

Before 1 December

  1. Send the welcome packWelcome letter, invoice and this roadmap to Fluid Equity.
  2. Name the leads on both sidesOne IBA lead, and one Fluid Equity lead (for example Andrew Lee, who runs operations and partnerships), to own the roadmap and the gates.
  3. Share the onboarding form and Membership AgreementSo both are complete by 5 December.
  4. Book the welcome callWeek of 7 December, with the co-founders.

On the welcome call

  1. Walk through this roadmapConfirm the three goals, the four stages, the Gate 1 criteria and the pathway through The Circle.
  2. Book Q1's three mentorship blocksDecember, January and February, four hours each.
  3. Agree how we workA short monthly check-in inside the mentorship hours; email updates between.
  4. Hand over the starter kitKPI framework, playbooks, templates and the data-room checklist.

What we need from Fluid Equity

  • Lead and billing contactsName, title, email and billing address.
  • Documents for the vaultPilot-fund deck, financial model, the Class A / Class B structure summary, and what counsel can share.
  • Spotlight materialLogo, a one-line description and the mission in a sentence: "The rent should leave something behind."
  • Where the pilot standsFund target and progress, buildings under review, legal milestones: the starting point for the baseline.
How the gates work. At the end of each quarter we review the gate together. When its criteria are met, the next stage opens; if something is outstanding, we agree what closes it and carry it into the next quarter's first weeks. The gates keep the year moving at Fluid Equity's pace: legal first, no rushing the foundation.
